# Product Overview

## Astra Codex - Novel Agent Frontend

A sophisticated AI-powered novel writing and analysis platform that helps authors create, analyze, and enhance their literary works through intelligent automation.

### Core Features

**Novel Analysis & Extraction**
- Import novels in multiple formats (.epub, .txt, .md)
- AI-powered extraction of characters, worldbuilding, plot elements, and golden fingers
- Relationship mapping and narrative structure analysis

**Creative Writing Tools**
- Interactive outline generation (novel → volume → chapter levels)
- AI-assisted chapter writing with review and revision cycles
- Real-time writing assistance with context-aware suggestions

**Element Fusion System**
- Extract abstract patterns from existing works
- Combine elements from multiple sources to create new settings
- Generate fusion candidates with originality scoring

**Project Management**
- Multi-project workspace with progress tracking
- Task-based workflow with real-time status updates
- Comprehensive analytics and statistics

### Target Users

- **Authors & Writers**: Professional and aspiring novelists seeking AI assistance
- **Content Creators**: Those working on serialized fiction or web novels
- **Literary Analysts**: Researchers studying narrative patterns and structures

### Key Value Propositions

1. **Accelerated Creation**: Reduce time from concept to publishable content
2. **Enhanced Creativity**: Discover new combinations and patterns through AI fusion
3. **Structured Workflow**: Systematic approach to novel planning and execution
4. **Quality Assurance**: Built-in review and consistency checking
